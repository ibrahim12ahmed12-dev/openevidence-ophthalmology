# Power calculation - paired exact McNemar test (assumption grid)

These are planning calculations from stated assumptions, not study results.

Minimum detectable absolute difference (percentage points) at 80% power, n = 421 paired cases:

| Discordance | alpha 0.05 | alpha 0.025 (Holm 2nd) | alpha 0.0167 (Holm 1st of 3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 4.5 | 5.0 | 5.3 |
| 15% | 5.5 | 6.0 | 6.2 |
| 20% | 6.5 | 7.0 | 7.3 |
| 25% | 7.0 | 7.8 | 8.2 |
| 30% | 7.8 | 8.5 | 9.0 |
| 35% | 8.5 | 9.2 | 9.5 |
| 40% | 9.0 | 9.8 | 10.2 |

Interpretation: at n = 421 the methodology's '6-8 percentage points' corresponds to discordance of roughly 15-30% at alpha 0.05, and roughly 15-25% at the Holm first-step alpha of 0.0167. With 35-40% discordance the detectable difference is about 9-10 points, and it grows further when fewer pairs are analysable (see the n = 380 and 340 rows). It is therefore a plausible but unverified figure until pilot discordance is known.
